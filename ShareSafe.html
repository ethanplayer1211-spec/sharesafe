HOW IT WORKS

Edit and export

  1. Open an imageChoose a PNG, JPG or WebP, drop it here, or paste it with Ctrl/⌘+V.
  2. Cover and annotateDrag to redact. Click to number. Use Select to move an edit or resize a cover from a corner.
  3. Review and exportHold Compare to check the original. Export saves a full-resolution PNG with the covers flattened into the image.

ShareSafe does not detect private details automatically. Review the whole image before sharing. Your edits stay in this tab and are lost when it closes.
